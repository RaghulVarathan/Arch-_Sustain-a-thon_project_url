# Neutral Forensic Terminology & Non-Accusatory Principles

## Mandatory Language Rules
1. **Never use accusatory language**:
   - Strictly forbidden: "fraud", "cheating", "fake data", "malicious tampering", "guilty factory".
   - Required neutral terminology:
     - "Anomaly detected"
     - "Unusual pattern observed"
     - "Requires verification"
     - "Investigation signal"
     - "Data inconsistency"
     - "Verification priority"

2. **Investigation-Support Mandate**:
   - An anomaly is a statistical or biological pattern deviation, NOT a legal conclusion.
   - Anomaly scores must never be claimed as a "scientifically validated probability of fraud" unless a legally calibrated probabilistic model is explicitly configured.

3. **Explainability Requirement**:
   - Every metric or score must answer:
     1. What was observed?
     2. When was it logged?
     3. Which specific data triggered the variance?
     4. Why is physical or sensor verification advised?
